# Apex Global Technologies - Employee Handbook & Operations Policy

## Chapter 1: Remote Work and Home Office Reimbursement
Apex Global Technologies supports a flexible hybrid workplace culture. Full-time employees are eligible for the following remote work benefits:

### Home Office Equipment Allowance:
- Every new employee receives a one-time reimbursement of up to $1,200 for ergonomic desk setup, dual monitors, or high-definition webcams.
- Receipts must be submitted within the first 60 days of employment via the corporate expense portal.
- Annual internet stipend: Employees working more than 3 days per week remotely receive a monthly credit of $75.

## Chapter 2: Information Security and Data Protection
All company laptops must run disk-level encryption (BitLocker or FileVault) and use two-factor authentication (2FA) for single sign-on (SSO).
- **Customer Data Protection**: Sensitive customer files (PII) must never be uploaded to unapproved public cloud drives or consumer AI tools.
- **VPN Requirement**: Connecting to production databases or internal staging clusters requires connecting through the Apex WireGuard VPN tunnel.

## Chapter 3: Paid Time Off (PTO) and Health Benefits
- **Vacation Time**: Full-time employees accrue 22 days of PTO per calendar year, increasing to 26 days after 3 years of service.
- **Sick Leave**: 10 paid sick days per year, separate from vacation PTO.
- **Parental Leave**: 16 weeks of 100% paid parental leave for primary and secondary caregivers, eligible from day one of employment.
- **Continuing Education Stipend**: $2,500 annually per employee for conferences, technical certifications, and books.
